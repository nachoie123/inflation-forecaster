Quantum · Project 02 — Macro Forecasting

Inflation Forecaster

A working paper that forecasts US inflation with a seasonal ARIMA model — and grades itself honestly against the baselines that are genuinely hard to beat.

Method · SARIMA on log-CPI Data · FRED, monthly Validation · walk-forward backtest

Pick an inflation measure. First run ≈ 10s — it downloads the series, fits 7 models by AIC and walk-forward backtests 36 months. Then cached.
